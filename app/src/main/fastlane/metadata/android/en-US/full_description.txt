Hovr is a powerful utility designed to enhance your workflow by providing floating overlays. It allows you to keep reference images, animated GIFs, and text notes always on top of other apps.

Key Features:
- Image Overlays: Keep your visual references visible at all times.
- GIF Support: Display animated references or decorations.
- Text Notes: Quick access to important information or reminders.
- Clean Interface: Minimalist design that stays out of your way.
