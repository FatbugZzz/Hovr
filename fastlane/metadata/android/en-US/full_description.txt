Hovr lets you float images and animated GIFs on top of any other app on your screen.

Open an image from your gallery or paste a URL, and it appears as a floating window you can drag anywhere, resize with a pinch, rotate, and control — all while keeping your other apps fully usable underneath.

Features:
- Up to 3 simultaneous overlays, each independently controllable
- Open images and GIFs from gallery or URL
- Drag, pinch to resize, and rotate overlays
- GIF playback speed control (0.25× to 4×)
- Opacity slider
- Position lock
- Bubble mode — collapse to a small circle
- Hide toolbar with long press
- Favorites and history
- Automatic night mode
- Home screen widget
- English and Spanish interface

No ads. No tracking. No internet required for local files.
